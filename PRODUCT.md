# Aster Journal
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
android

## Stack
Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Material 3. Native Android only; no WebView or remote website in the APK. Local-first Room database and DataStore preferences. The user delegated design and engineering decisions.

## Users
People capturing everyday thoughts and exploring deeper personal reflection on an Android phone. Indonesian is the primary interface language. POCO F6 running Android 16 is the requested physical acceptance device.

## Product Purpose
Make starting, saving, organizing, revisiting, and owning private writing effortless. Success is a useful installed application, not a mockup.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Notes, checklists, guided reflection, optional moods, folders, tags, pin/favorite, archive/trash, search, JSON backup/import and readable Markdown. All writing stays in app-private storage; no account, analytics, Internet permission, cloud, or AI service. Never claim database encryption or clinical benefits. Photos are optional and bounded. Destructive removal is explicit. Existing package identity is com.wynn.jurnalbintang. Old browser storage requires explicit JSON migration; Android cannot directly read a remote website's localStorage.

## Brand Commitments
Aster Journal; original white/blue identity with a designed dark scheme, calm editorial headings, legible controls, native Android interactions. Three supplied Dribbble/Behance references inspire original work; their artwork is not licensed for redistribution.

## Evidence on Hand
The preserved prior app is in legacy/web. Product research, supplied reference observations, architecture decisions, and verification results live in docs. No real journal content or credentials may enter research, graph outputs, logs, tests, or screenshots.

## Product Principles
- Writing and data integrity outrank decoration and feature count.
- Local actions work without connectivity.
- Privacy claims match implemented capabilities.
- Use familiar Android controls and accessible touch targets.
- Empty states invite writing without fabricating personal history.
